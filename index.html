02 / HOW I GOT HERE

Still curious.

My story started with a degree in agriculture in Nepal. I liked being out in the field, asking questions, and discovering how much there was still to understand.

Along the way, I got interested in GIS, drones, and what data can tell us about agriculture. I moved to the US in August 2026 and recently began my MS in Natural Resource Sciences at North Dakota State University, with a focus on precision agriculture.

I’m still finding my direction, trying new tools, and learning from the people and projects around me. This website is a little collection of that journey, with plenty more to come.
