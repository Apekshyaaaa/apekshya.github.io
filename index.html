01 / ABOUT

Research rooted
in real fields.

My background began in agricultural research in Nepal, where I studied orchard management and farm-level production challenges. That experience led me toward using spatial data and remote sensing to examine agricultural systems from a different perspective.

Today, I'm pursuing an M.S. in Natural Resource Sciences, with a Precision Agriculture sub-plan, at North Dakota State University. I'm interested in the connection between field conditions, UAV imagery, model performance, and practical decision-making.
