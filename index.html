02 / A LITTLE MORE ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.

I studied agriculture at Agriculture and Forestry University in Nepal, where I learned to ask questions about farms, farmers, and the systems around them.

That curiosity eventually led me toward spatial data, drones, and research. Now I'm pursuing an MS in Natural Resource Sciences (Precision Agriculture) at North Dakota State University.

I like work that connects what's happening on the ground with what we can learn from data. And I'm always up for learning a new tool.
