02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Rooted in agriculture.
Open to possibilities.

I studied agriculture at Agriculture and Forestry University in Nepal, where field research and data analysis became important parts of my work.

My interests gradually grew to include maps, remote sensing, and drones. In August 2026, I moved to the United States to begin my MS in Natural Resource Sciences, with a precision agriculture sub-plan, at North Dakota State University.

I'm interested in learning how these tools work together, and in finding new questions worth asking along the way.
