01 / THE PERSON

A LITTLE CONTEXT

Grounded in agriculture.
Curious about what's next.

↗NEPAL → NORTH DAKOTA

My path started with agricultural field research in Nepal. Today, I'm an MS student in Natural Resource Sciences at North Dakota State University, with a precision agriculture focus.

I am interested in how UAV imagery, GIS, and machine-learning methods can help us understand what is happening in a field — and whether those methods still work when the field, season, or crop stage changes.

Beyond technical work, I care about asking practical questions, evaluating evidence carefully, and making research understandable.
